How to play

👆
SteerDrag left and right anywhere on the screen. The squad runs and shoots by itself.
🟦
Pick your gatesBlue gates add or multiply soldiers. Red gates take them away. Weapon gates upgrade every gun in the squad.
🎯
Shoot the red gatesBullets push a gate's number up. Fire into a −12 long enough and it turns into a +5.
📦
Crates and barrelsShoot crates open for soldiers and weapons. Shoot red barrels to blow up the horde around them. Don't run into either.
🧟
The hordeEach zombie that reaches the squad takes soldiers with it. Brutes keep chewing. Spitters lob acid: steer out of the red circle.
✈️
Airstrike and shieldKills charge the airstrike. The shield (unlock it at HQ) blocks all damage for a few seconds.
💀
BossesEvery stage ends with a boss. Watch for red warning circles on the road.
