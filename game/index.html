How to play

☝️
SwimTouch anywhere and drag. The farther you drag, the faster you swim. Keyboard: WASD or arrows.
➤
DashQuick tap, second finger, or the Dash button. You are invulnerable while dashing, and a dash rams creatures too big to eat. Space on keyboard.
◎
SonarReveals everything nearby and stuns it. Bosses with shields drop them when pinged. E on keyboard.
👁️
Read the eyesGreen eyes: you can eat it. Red eyes: it can eat you.
💡
Light is lifeYour light drains over time and when you get bitten. Eat to keep it burning. A brighter lantern shows more, but predators see you from farther away.
🧬
EvolveEating gives XP. Each level lets you pick a mutation. Pearls buy permanent upgrades between dives.
⚠️
GuardiansSomething waits at 1000 m, 3000 m and 6000 m. Dash into them, and use sonar on shields.
