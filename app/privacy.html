Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026.

gta6 uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the unofficial GTA 6 fan page @gta6.222.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses one Google permission: uploading videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope). It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other users, and it does not access anyone else's Google account.

What it stores

Only the sign-in token for the owner's channel, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository so uploads can run automatically. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: send a direct message to @gta6.222 on Instagram.
